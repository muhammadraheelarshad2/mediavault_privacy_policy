Privacy Policy

MediaVault is designed as a local-first video player, download manager and private media organizer. This policy explains what information the app accesses, when network services are involved, and how you can control your data.

Effective date: September 10, 2026 App: MediaVault 1.0 Contact: info@hscreatorssoftware.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the MediaVault Android application. It describes the current app's privacy practices and should be read together with any terms presented through Google Play or inside the app.

MediaVault does not require an account for its core local media experience.

2. Information MediaVault handles

Media and files on your device

When you grant media access, MediaVault can read supported videos and related file information so it can display, organize, play, scan and manage media. Downloaded files are saved to device storage according to Android's storage framework and your choices.

Download and media metadata

The app may keep local records needed to manage downloads, such as file name, source/platform label, media type, duration, size, status, timestamps and local file references. These records support the Downloads, Recent and media-management screens.

Links you provide

When you paste or open a supported media link, MediaVault processes the URL to identify the media source and, when required, resolve downloadable media. A URL you submit can therefore be transmitted to the app's configured resolver service or to the relevant media website/service.

Local-first: your core video library and private Vault content are designed to remain on your device. MediaVault does not require you to create a profile for those features.

3. Network access, browser use and downloads

MediaVault uses internet access for features that need online content, including browsing web pages, resolving supported media links, previewing remote media and downloading media.

Resolver service

For supported social or remote media, the app may send the submitted URL to its configured MediaVault resolver service. The resolver can return media variants, download URLs and related technical information required to complete the requested operation.

Network providers and websites may also receive normal technical information associated with an internet request, such as IP address, request time, device/network information and standard HTTP information. Their own privacy policies and terms govern that processing.

Built-in browser

If you use MediaVault's browser, web content is supplied by third-party websites. Those websites may use cookies, local storage, scripts or other technologies under their own policies. MediaVault does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

4. Android permissions

  • Video/media access: used to show and manage videos available on your device and to import videos into MediaVault.
  • Audio/media access: used where Android media access is needed for supported media functionality.
  • Notifications: used to show download progress and download status on supported Android versions.
  • Internet/network state: used for online media resolution, browser features, playback of remote media and downloads.

You can review or revoke Android permissions at any time from the app's Android settings page. Some features may stop working when their required permission is unavailable.

5. Private Vault

Videos imported into the MediaVault Private Vault are stored in the app's private storage and encrypted using Android Keystore-backed cryptographic keys. The Vault is intended to keep protected media separate from ordinary device media access.

Vault content is not uploaded to a MediaVault account because the app does not require an account for the Vault. Temporary playback files may be created inside the app's private cache while protected content is being played and are subject to automatic cleanup.

Important: no software can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Android device protected with a secure screen lock and install updates from trusted sources.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

MediaVault keeps local app data for as long as needed to provide the features you use, unless you remove it sooner. Download history, local metadata and Vault content can be removed through the app's available controls. Android's app-data controls can also be used to clear app data.

Uninstalling MediaVault normally removes its private app storage. Files that you explicitly saved to shared/device storage may remain until you delete them yourself.

When an online resolver or third-party website is involved, retention of server-side network logs or submitted URLs is controlled by that service's operational practices and applicable policy. MediaVault does not claim control over retention by independent third-party services.

7. Sharing and disclosure

MediaVault does not sell your personal information. The app does not require a user account or maintain a social profile for the core app experience.

Information may be processed by service providers when technically necessary to deliver a feature you request, such as a media resolver, web host or the network provider serving remote content. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

When you use Android's Share feature, the content you choose to share is handed to the Android sharing mechanism and the application you select. That recipient's privacy policy applies to its subsequent processing.

8. Security

MediaVault uses Android platform security controls, private app storage and encryption for protected Vault content. Network requests to configured online services are made over supported HTTP/HTTPS connections; secure HTTPS should be used whenever the destination supports it.

Security is a shared responsibility. Do not paste passwords, private credentials or other secrets into media URLs or public web pages.

9. Children

MediaVault is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app's core experience is local-first and does not require an account, it does not intentionally create child profiles. Parents or guardians should supervise use of online browsing and third-party media services where appropriate.

10. Your choices and controls

  • Grant only the Android media permissions you are comfortable providing.
  • Revoke permissions from Android Settings at any time.
  • Remove download history entries using the app's management controls.
  • Delete downloaded files when you no longer need them.
  • Remove protected Vault items from the Vault when desired.
  • Clear MediaVault app data or uninstall the app to remove private app data stored by Android.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MediaVault's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.
